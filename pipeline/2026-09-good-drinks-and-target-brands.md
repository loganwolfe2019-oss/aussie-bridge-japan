## PART 1 — Un-sticking Good Drinks Australia

### What actually happened
Met the GM of Sales. It stalled because GDA wanted the Japanese distributor's
**first purchase to be a full container load (FCL)**, and the distributor
wouldn't commit to that volume cold. Both sides want the other to carry the
risk on the first order. That's the whole problem — and it's solvable.

### The reframe
Don't argue about who commits to the container. Change the structure so
**nobody carries a full container of risk on one brand.**

Three mechanisms, best first:

**1. Consolidated container (ABJ's real edge).**
The distributor commits to a full container — but it's shared across 3–5
Australian brands, not just GDA. GDA still ships in a "real container",
the distributor's exposure to any single brand is a few pallets. This is
something Austrade or a freight forwarder can't easily offer; it only works
because ABJ is bringing multiple brands at once.

**2. Trial-to-FCL roadmap.**
First shipment is LCL (a few pallets) into named buyers to prove sell-through.
Written agreement that the FCL follows on a set date once the trial hits an
agreed number. GDA gets a committed path to the container, not a vague "maybe
later".

**3. Capped downside on the first FCL.**
If the distributor does take a full container of GDA alone, GDA shares the
risk back — consignment terms on that first container, or a buy-back / credit
on anything unsold after 12 months. The distributor commits because the
worst case is defined.

Lead the conversation with **1 + 2**. Keep **3** in your back pocket.

### Who to send it to
The GM of Sales is your channel, but per what we know, **the export decision
at GDA sits with the owner/founder**. So: reply to the GM, give them a clean
one-pager they can walk into the founder's office with. Make it easy to say
yes upward.

## PART 2 — Target brand profile (the filter)

A brand is worth ABJ's time if it clears most of these:

| Test | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Founder/owner-led, smaller-to-mid | The person who says yes to export is reachable |
| Premium enough to absorb freight + distributor margin + ABJ cut | If it's cheap in Australia it's unsellable in Japan after the stack |
| Product travels (shelf-stable, or simple cold chain) | Kills most fresh/frozen unless they're already exporting |
| "Australian" is an asset for the category | Clean/green, natural, provenance — Japan pays for the story |
| Regulatory path isn't a wall | Alcohol, health claims, some additives = slow. Know before pitching |
| Not already established in Japan | Nothing to broker if they're in |
| A story + consistent supply | Japanese buyers rate story and reliability over price |

If it fails "premium enough" or "product travels", stop — those two kill deals.

>

**Rules for filling it in:**
- The `[specific, true reason]` line is the whole email. Generic = deleted.
- Never name other brands you're talking to.

  deliberate work — it builds trust and it's true.
