# FOODEX Japan 2027 — plan
_9–12 March 2027, Tokyo Big Sight. Drafted 10 Sep 2026 (~6 months out)._

---

## Why go
- Meet Japanese buyers and importers in volume — hundreds in one hall.
- Meet Australian brands weighing up Japan — the Austrade Australian
  pavilion is a pipeline in one place.
- See the category from the Japanese side — what's on shelf, what's missing,
  what it's priced at.
- Be seen. A new operator turning up to FOODEX is a credibility signal.

## Go as a visitor, not an exhibitor (recommendation)
At your stage — no signed client yet, near-zero budget — a stand isn't
justified: it costs money you don't have and needs product to show. Go as a
visitor (registration is free or low-cost). If the distributor has a stand,
ask to spend time on it and be introduced to who comes by. Revisit
exhibiting for FOODEX 2028 if you've got 2–3 signed brands by then.

## Decision 1 — trip dates (do this first)
Current plan: Japan two weeks, February 2027. FOODEX is 9–12 March.
- **Best:** extend or shift the trip to cover 9–12 March. One flight, both jobs.
- If the dates genuinely can't move: a separate short March trip for FOODEX
  only, or skip 2027 and target 2028.
- Don't fly to Japan in February and miss FOODEX by a week.
- **Needed from you by early October** to book flights at a sane price.

## Decision 2 — the day job
Two to three weeks in Japan across February–March is hard to explain at Moco.
Work out the leave plan now, not in January. This is the constraint most
likely to derail the trip.
