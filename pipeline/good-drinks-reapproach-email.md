## If they push back

- **"The first order's too small to bother our production line."**
  → The step-up schedule is the answer — show the [timeframe] to full
  container volume. The small order is the on-ramp, not the destination.

- **"How do we know the distributor will actually scale up?"**
  → That's why the schedule is written and tied to a sell-through trigger,
  not a promise. Offer to have the trigger numbers agreed by all three
  parties before anything ships.

- **"We want more certainty on volume before we commit."**
  → Fallback lever: consignment terms or a buy-back clause on unsold stock
  from the first full container, so their downside is capped. Only offer
  this if the staged plan alone isn't landing.
