About Us

Bridging Two Worlds With Precision and Purpose

Our Story

Founded on a Simple Conviction

Aussie Bridge Japan was founded on a simple conviction: Australian brands produce world-class products that deserve a place in Japan's premium market — but getting there requires more than ambition alone.

We are a newly launched, founder-led agency built on real connections on both sides of the Pacific. Our focus is on building genuine, long-term partnerships with brands ready to enter Japan — and growing together.

Our Japan Network

Real Infrastructure in Japan

Aussie Bridge Japan is backed by real infrastructure in Japan. Our Japanese distribution partner is a wholesale distributor headquartered in Nagoya, operating a distribution hub and an established partner network that reaches hospitality venues, retailers, and buyers across Japan. When a brand joins our portfolio, it doesn't enter Japan through a third party — it enters through our own network, managed by people who understand the Japanese market from the inside.
