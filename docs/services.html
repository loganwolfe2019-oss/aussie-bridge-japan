02

Compliance Coordination

We coordinate all regulatory compliance and documentation for seamless entry into Japan. Our Japan distribution partner BEST provides approved Japanese label templates for each product. Aussie Bridge Japan communicates these templates to the brand. The brand prints and applies the labels to their product before shipment — ensuring every unit arrives in Japan fully compliant and ready for customs clearance.

  • Regulatory compliance & documentation
  • Japanese label templates provided by BEST — brands apply before shipment
  • Quarantine & food safety guidance
  • Cold chain logistics coordination

03

Distribution Introductions

Through our family partner BEST, a Japanese wholesale distributor headquartered in Nagoya, we facilitate access to an established distribution network — warehousing, logistics, and last-mile delivery to venues and retailers across Japan.

  • Nagoya distribution hub via family partner BEST
  • BEST's established partner network — national reach
  • Last-mile delivery to venues, retailers, hospitality groups
  • Starts in Nagoya, expands nationally as volume grows

Commercial Model

Pure-Agency Brand Management

ABJ operates as your brand's exclusive Japanese representative — not as an importer, trader, or stock holder. We manage market relationships, compliance coordination, and distribution introductions on your behalf.

Our fee structure typically comprises an onboarding fee to establish your brand in the market, and a monthly brand management retainer. Specific terms are agreed on a brand-by-brand basis.

A

Onboarding Fee

One-time Japan market entry setup — compliance review, distribution introductions, brand positioning.

B

Monthly Retainer

Ongoing brand management, market development, and account servicing.

Actual fee amounts are confirmed during the assessment stage. Contact us for an indicative proposal.
