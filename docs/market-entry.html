The Challenge

Why Most Brands Struggle Without Local Expertise

01

Regulatory Complexity

Japan has some of the most detailed food and beverage import regulations in the world, including strict labelling requirements in Japanese.

02

Distribution Structure

The Japanese distribution system is multi-layered and relationship-driven. Without local connections, gaining shelf space is nearly impossible.

03

Cultural Nuances

Consumer preferences, packaging expectations, and marketing approaches differ significantly from Western markets.

04

Language Barrier

All business negotiations, regulatory filings, and marketing materials must be in Japanese to be effective.
